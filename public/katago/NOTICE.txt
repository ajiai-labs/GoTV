KataGo analysis in GoTV
=======================

katago-worker.js
  The KataGo engine of Web KaTrain (https://github.com/Sir-Teo/web-katrain,
  commit a6a67d6), MIT licence - see LICENSE-web-katrain.txt - bundled with
  TensorFlow.js 4.22.0 (Apache-2.0) and pako 2.1.0 (MIT) by
  tools/katago-worker/build.sh. Licence texts of the bundled libraries are
  kept at the end of katago-worker.js.

tfjs/*.wasm
  TensorFlow.js WASM backend 4.22.0, Apache-2.0.

models/katago-small.bin.gz
  KataGo network g170-b6c96-s175395328-d26788732, as shipped by Web KaTrain.
  KataGo networks: see https://katagotraining.org/network_license/ (the g170
  networks are released into the public domain / CC0 there).

A stronger network (e.g. kata1-b18c384nbt from katagotraining.org) can be
loaded from disk in the Nachspielen tab; it stays in the browser.
